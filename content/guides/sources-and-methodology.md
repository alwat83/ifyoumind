## Bill checks

A bill check compares two property-tax bills or two electricity bills. You can try a made-up example, type the amounts from your own two bills, or use one of two supported CSV formats. PDFs, scans and other file types are not supported.

- **Where the numbers come from:** only the two bills you provide, or the made-up example. No external data is fetched.
- **How findings are calculated:** each finding shows the exact source cells it used and the arithmetic, so you can check every step.
- **Where the calculation runs:** in your browser tab. Bill figures are not uploaded to ifYouMind, sent to an AI provider or saved to browser storage. Clearing your data, changing bill type or leaving the page discards them from the experience.
- **What two bills cannot tell you:** why the amounts changed, whether an assessment is correct, or what comes next. Two bills show one comparison, not a trend. For electricity, total billed divided by kWh is a whole-bill average, not your tariff, and fixed and variable charges are identified only from explicit bill labels.

## Homepage examples

The property-tax and electricity examples use made-up numbers. Raw values and source cells come from the made-up example CSV. The statements you can check are preset for each example, and each verdict is calculated from that example's numbers. A verdict describes those numbers only. It is not proof that a real bill is right or wrong.

The city example uses figures from the public [sample report](/sample-report).

## City briefs and Decision Reports

City briefs and the $79 Decision Report use [U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimates](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.html) for population, median household income and median home value.

- **Population change** compares the 2015–2019 and 2020–2024 ACS 5-year estimates.
- **Differences between places** have not been tested for statistical significance and may fall within survey margins of error.
- **Each key figure** links to its official source table.
- **What the figures describe:** the people who live in each place. They do not measure demand for your business, competitors, commercial rent or foot traffic, so on their own they cannot recommend a location.
- **The sample report** uses real ACS estimates retrieved for that example. The decision in it is illustrative.

Paid reports use public city and market evidence. They do not analyze a household bill, verify a property's tax assessment, or establish that you are overpaying.

## Optional AI evidence summary

Where it is available, the city-findings evidence summary runs only after you choose it. Only public Census metric names, figures, numbered place aliases and a broad decision category are sent. Your question, city names, account identity, bills and uploaded documents are excluded. ifYouMind supplies the displayed figures, sources, wording and limitations. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
