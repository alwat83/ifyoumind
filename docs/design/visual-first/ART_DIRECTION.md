# Visual-first discovery — November 4, 2026

OpenAI / Codex. Founder requested a modern, clean, simple, imagery-led and playful experience with less reading and less friction between comprehension, trying the product and eventually choosing a paid offering. This explicitly authorizes a feature-branch redesign; release and outbound gates remain separate.

## Direction

Warm ivory, deep violet, lavender, chartreuse and a small peach accent. Bold contemporary type, generous space, sculptural paper-and-chart imagery. The lead is a familiar bill comparison, not a speculative arbitrary-file or AI capability. Interactive synthetic previews make the value visible. One primary hero action opens the actual electricity findings without a signup or second category selection. The property-tax preview links to its actual example. No autoplay, simulated chat, fabricated savings, testimonials or invented traction.

The homepage keeps Business sample access and optional Free/$79 one-time/$39 monthly Pro offers, including five full reports per month and preserved report-registration intent. Bill discovery remains free, distinct from Business monetization. The broader general-purpose vision remains; this slice shows what works today. Guides remain available from the footer.

Discovery entry and shared result surfaces use the same palette. Mobile acceptance includes first-screen access to both example buttons, readable findings, accessible keyboard controls, evidence expansion, no horizontal overflow, correction/retry/clear and no new persistence or service calls. Existing two recognized USD CSV restrictions and noindex/private isolation remain.
