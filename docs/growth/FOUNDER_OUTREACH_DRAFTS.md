# Founder-led buyer validation — drafts only

Prepared 2026-10-22. **Not sent.** No contacts, partnerships or purchases are claimed. Founder approval is required before external customer/partner communications. Use a relevant existing relationship or an appropriate permission-based context; do not mass-send or purchase lists. Link only live, verified capabilities; new guides/growth features await separate deployment approval.

## Qualification and short conversation

Seek a person actively comparing U.S. cities for a business opening/expansion or preparing a business plan, where Census context is useful. Ask: What decision are you making, by when? How do you compare options today? Which evidence is missing? What would make a report worth $79? Does this sample help, and what would prevent using it? Record volunteered feedback without unnecessary personal data. Site competition, rent and financial forecasting are outside the current evidence scope.

Suggested invitation, subject to founder approval and personal editing:

> Are you currently comparing cities for a business location or business plan? I’m developing ifYouMind, which turns cited public demographic evidence into a location Decision Brief and an optional $79 Decision Report. There’s a full sample you can review without signing up: https://ifyoumind.com/sample-report. If this matches a decision you’re making, would you be open to a short demonstration and candid feedback? It supports research; it doesn’t predict sales or replace site-level due diligence.

## Seven-minute demonstration

1. Ask about the actual decision and confirm geographic/data fit. Stop if the tool does not answer the customer's question.
2. Show the public sample's recommendation, comparisons, sources and limitations. Demonstrate existing copy/print only when that capability is live and verified.
3. Explain the free brief (one comparison), optional $79 full report (up to three), and $39/month Pro with five included monthly reports when repeated use makes sense. No invented discount, scarcity, testimonials or guarantees.
4. Show registration and the free workflow without creating a real transaction. Let the customer decide independently whether to buy; never simulate a purchase and count it as revenue.
5. Ask what was useful, what was missing, whether they would pay and why. Record stated willingness separately from actual confirmed payment.

## Measurement record

For each approved activity, record date, anonymous prospect code, segment/current decision, source, sample reviewed, demo completed, stated objection, next permissioned action and verified purchase reference if available. Keep contact details in an approved private system, not Git. Count unique independently funded buyers only after payment reconciliation; founder/QA accounts, test-mode checkouts and stated intent are excluded. No channel ROI or conversion rate is established by these drafts.
