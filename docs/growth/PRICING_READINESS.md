Fresh read-only Stripe evidence in the verified live account matches the production GitHub variables. Both monthly USD prices are active, licensed and have no trial set. The enabled dedicated subscription endpoint targets the deployed function and listens for paid invoices, subscription updates/deletions, refunds, and dispute creation/update/closure on `2025-02-24.acacia`. Its return URL is `https://ifyoumind.com/app/account`. The founder previously confirmed that the signing secret version was created. A deployed function and enabled destination do not establish successful signed delivery or least-privilege runtime Stripe access. 3. **Authorize a separate enrollment change for one named workspace.** A reviewed workflow change must replace the forced-empty input with the masked approved allowlist. Verify the first independent payment, invoice grant, saved report, Account credit and owner portal return before expanding. A public default for every new workspace requires a later explicit decision to replace the named-cohort gate. Until then these are the primary offers within the approved cohort.

**Pause or roll back.** Close the allowlist through the approved deployment path. Keep the subscription webhook and portal servicing paid customers, and preserve saved reports. Reconcile open sessions and settled invoices before any rollback of server code. An exact prior dormant revision is suitable for an approved pre-enrollment rollback.
