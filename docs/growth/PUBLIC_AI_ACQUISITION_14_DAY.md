## Claim boundary and landing destination

The live homepage demonstrates an **illustrative** $420 property-tax increase, including a $240 school-tax contribution. Those are made-up example figures, not a customer's result, a local tax rate or proof of overpayment. Discovery calculates supported property-tax/electricity comparisons locally from recognized CSVs. It does not accept arbitrary bills/PDFs or explain every cause. Private bill findings are not sent to OpenAI. Optional, consented AI selects an evidence-backed summary of supported **saved city findings**, using public Census figures and a coarse category; it is not property-tax AI, a chatbot or a promise of personal savings. The $79 city/business report is separate from free household bill analysis. Monthly enrollment remains unavailable.

Destination: [existing homepage](https://ifyoumind.com/), with its approved design and copy unchanged. Campaign message: **“Your tax bill went up. See which numbers changed.”** CTA: **“Reveal what changed.”** After the reveal: inspect evidence, then use the existing supported private CSV analysis if appropriate. Do not imply that a city report diagnoses a household tax bill. A separate city-oriented post may demonstrate the verified AI summary only after the new eligibility release/canary is recorded; never overlay “AI analyzed this bill.”

## Two carousel concepts

Use 4:5 editorial slides with one message per slide, legible source/illustration disclosures, and no fabricated outcome or appeal promise.

**Carousel A — “Where did the extra $420 go?”**

1. “Your tax bill went up. Where did the extra $420 go?” / “Illustrative example · made-up figures.”
2. “$240 of this example increase is school tax.” / Actual matching evidence crop, not a recreated unexplained number.
3. “Check the arithmetic.” / Show the existing before/after subtraction and contribution calculation; preserve units/periods and example label.
4. “A change is not proof of an error.” / “The figures show what changed, not whether an assessment is correct or appealable.”
5. “Reveal what changed.” / “Try the free example at ifYouMind.com. No account needed for the example.”

**Carousel B — “Know what the answer can tell you.”**

1. “A useful answer should come with evidence.”
2. “Bills: compare supported figures and periods.” / Synthetic property-tax/electricity screenshot; label example figures.
3. “Cities: compare sourced public estimates.” / City example/source, “not your personal expenses.”
4. “Private bill comparisons stay in the browser.” / Clarify this describes Discovery, not all account features; no claim of zero provider processing across the whole website.
5. “Go deeper only when it fits your question.” / “Free bill analysis and paid city/business Decision Reports serve different needs. Start with a free example.” Do not advertise closed subscriptions as purchasable.

## Privacy-safe measurement proposal — no tracking implementation

Use existing consented analytics only where actually implemented; otherwise a voluntary anonymous facilitator tally. Do not transmit events from private Discovery to defeat its no-network boundary. No question text, city pair, salary, bill numbers, file metadata, addresses, identity, workspace/project IDs, AI request IDs, payment details or persistent cross-site IDs in campaign analytics.

| Funnel observation | Proposed minimal aggregate | Evidence / counting rule |
|---|---|---|
| Landing visit | Daily channel-level visit count, only with applicable analytics consent | Exclude known internal QA/bots; unknown until measured. No fingerprinting. |
| Example reveal / evidence opened | Aggregate example category and event type on the public sample, if consent permits | Count one event per page view; do not infer comprehension from a click. Not a new implemented event. |
| Meaningful experience | Voluntary anonymized “one finding + one limitation” tally | Record numerator/denominator; never bill values or respondent identity. |
| City brief / optional explanation | Existing operational success/fallback counts, coarse latency/token totals | Keep quota/job identifiers private. Exclude internal canaries from customer counts. Consent is opt-in per AI action. |
| Paid city/business report | Reconciled payment, entitlement and fulfillment aggregate | Redirect/Checkout creation is not payment. Deduplicate by private provider ID; exclude internal/test/refunded activity when reporting independent customers. |

Do not claim a fully instrumented campaign funnel. Record actual provider cost separately from the conservative application budget reservation. Unknown visitors, customer understanding, revenue and contribution margin remain unknown.

## Launch recommendation

Prepare distribution now; publish only through founder-approved channels/content. Lead with deterministic tax value already available, rather than promising bill AI. Verify the new reader-role production canary separately before claiming expanded AI eligibility. Keep subscription enrollment closed. A campaign hypothesis is that a concrete, checkable finding earns more useful visits than abstract AI positioning; it remains untested until real, consenting people use the product. Paid promotion is not part of this plan.
