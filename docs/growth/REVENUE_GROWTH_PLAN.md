## Mission and available value

Launch an appealing, understandable, useful consumer-first product; get real people to try it; earn the first independent sale; learn and improve what matters. Long-term vision: anyone can make better decisions using real evidence. Business Decision Reports are the first monetization opportunity, not the company's entire identity.

The consolidated candidate offers four-choice discovery, four free decision guides, a complete public Business sample with sources/limits and print, the existing registered free Business brief, **$79 one-time Decision Reports** and **$39/month Pro with five included monthly reports**. Free brief: primary city plus one comparison; full report supports up to three comparisons. Personal automated calculators, arbitrary AI chat and uploads are not available. No experimental Life outputs exposed. Sources give city-level Census context, not site-level demand, rent, competition, sales forecasts or investment guarantees.
