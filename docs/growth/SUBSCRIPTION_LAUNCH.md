## Customer and billing contract

Free discovery and the public sample remain open. An eligible signed-in owner first sees a one-comparison sourced brief. From that result, they can choose a subscription or a $79 one-time purchase for the same full report. Starter includes one report for $4.99 per paid month. Plus includes three for $9.99. Both renew monthly, expire unused credits at the period end, allow cancellation before renewal from Account, and retain unlocked reports. The $3.99 experiment is deferred. Public and outside-cohort pricing retain existing offers. Legacy $39 Pro keeps its existing five-report UTC-month behavior and existing reports.

Checkout accepts a verified owner/admin, an existing workspace project, an eligible workspace, and a verified account/mode, active monthly USD Price and exact amount. A persisted checkout lease and provider idempotency key prevent duplicate starts and price swaps. Launch workspaces serialize subscription or $79 Checkout against report preparation on the same project. A single recorded account/mode cohort has a ten-workspace lifetime checkout-seat cap. It starts empty.

The separate signed webhook retrieves canonical Stripe invoice, subscription, Checkout and payment objects using API version `2025-02-24.acacia`. It accepts matching, successful positive collected invoices at the exact catalog amount. An idempotent Firestore transaction records the paid period, charge and receipt. Failed renewals, redirects, pending Checkout, invalid signatures, unsupported invoice shapes and wrong-mode objects do not grant credits. Canonical subscription state provides cancellation dates. Full reversal or an unresolved dispute holds unused credits, while previously unlocked reports stay readable. [Stripe invoices](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/object?api-version=2025-02-24.acacia) · [Stripe subscription webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks)

Report work is limited to one active reservation per workspace, at most three generation attempts per included credit, and three attempts per purchased deferred report. A free preview uses at most two locations and no full report generation. Participating free workspaces are capped at three preview attempts per UTC month. Linked workspaces have at most twelve. A full report fetches at most four distinct requested places with two ACS vintages and up to three attempts per vintage. Generation has an 80-second function timeout and a 90-second reservation lease. The credit is spent atomically with the saved report. Failed, expired or stale-token work leaves no grant or visible unlock. Existing report markers bypass further credit spending. Self-serve plan switching and re-enrollment after cancellation remain outside the initial launch.
