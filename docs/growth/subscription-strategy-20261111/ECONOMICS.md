Cloud Billing line items, attributable AI bills, project SKU usage, support labor and a current eligible purchase cohort were unavailable in this review. No actual cost per report or profitability result is claimed. Before paid exposure, meter successful and failed attempts separately, by warm or cold cache, then allocate free work and shared costs to the eligible cohort. Reconcile actual Stripe fee lines and unrecovered failures, reversals and dispute costs to net receipts. Track support minutes at a stated loaded hourly rate. Capture invoice period, region, free credits and exclusions so utilization or free-tier credits do not masquerade as marginal savings.

For a scale check only, use 25 reads, 8 writes, 10 vCPU-seconds, 5 GiB-seconds, 10 invocations and 1 MiB of Hosting transfer. Illustrative unit rates of $0.06 and $0.18 per 100,000 reads and writes, $0.000024 per vCPU-second, $0.0000025 per GiB-second, $0.40 per million invocations and $0.15/GB of Hosting transfer give about **$0.00044** before free credits, storage, logging, extra egress, cold retries, free traffic or labor. This is a hypothetical metered-resource floor for comparison, with rates varying by region and execution model. It is not a measured total or a substitute for the base-case reserve. ## Model inputs and interpretation

- Monthly domestic online card payment at 2.9% + $0.30, plus 0.7% pay-as-you-go Billing on collected volume. The tariff assumes no special contract, international surcharge or FX. Original processing costs remain in the model after refunds. [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/pricing) · [Stripe Billing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing)
- Assume 2% of billed invoices fail and stay unrecovered, 1% of collected payments are fully refunded, and 0.1% are separate lost disputes. Subtract disputed principal and a $15 received fee. A contested lost case can incur another $15. The model conservatively still allocates full delivery costs to billed accounts. At a 1% dispute rate and $30 total fee, expected dispute fees alone are about $0.29 per billed account plus principal losses. [Stripe dispute pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing#disputes)
- Variable support $0.35/account-month, equivalent to 0.6 minutes at $35/hour, and $0.15/account-month for shared delivery, including allocated free traffic. Both are reserves. Model $0.50/report to cover incremental delivery uncertainty, then stress at $0.20, $1, $1.50 and $2. Low, expected and max usage are 25%, 60% and 100%, averaged across subscribers. A subscriber's individual report count is integer.
- Cash receipts = billed price × collection rate × (1 − refund rate − dispute rate). Contribution = cash receipts − card fee − Billing fee − dispute fee − support − shared delivery − delivered reports × cost per report. Contribution margin uses gross billed price. Report cost ceiling = contribution before report cost divided by credits. At the 60% guardrail subtract 60% of price before dividing. These are contribution figures before fixed overhead, growth CAC, taxes and owner compensation.
- **$3.99/1** gives $2.42 or 60.6% at maximum usage with base assumptions. It falls below 60% above about $0.52 per report. **$14.99/8** falls below 60% above about $0.52 and loses $2.81 per billed month at $2 per report and full usage. Heavy usage can select into this tier. The two recommended pilot plans can tolerate roughly $0.84–$0.86 per full report before breaching the same guardrail. Paid enrichment or AI at $1.50 per report erodes all tiers, even before more support. Do not subsidize a new provider with assumed breakage.
- Model growth at a 70% Starter / 30% Plus mix. This gives $6.49 gross MRR per billed account and 1.6 maximum reports. At 100 / 500 / 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 accounts, MRR is $649 / $3,245 / $6,490 / $32,450 / $64,900. The [growth table](MODEL_OUTPUT.md#subscriber-scale-at-a-70-starter-and-30-plus-mix) shows net receipts, contribution, volume and replacement spend. One-time revenue is excluded. Free traffic and report concurrency need observing at 100–500. At 1,000+ check cache/retry load and support capacity, then reassess API quota, queue contention and hosting traffic at 5,000–10,000. At 10,000 and 6% churn, 600 monthly replacements are required to hold volume. The model does not establish an acquisition channel capable of that volume.
- Base CAC is $15 and churn 6% monthly. Use blended contribution divided by churn for simple undiscounted contribution LTV and CAC divided by monthly contribution for payback. With modeled contribution of $4.77, LTV is about $80 and payback 3.1 months. The stress case has about $24 LTV and $35 CAC. This is a sensitivity calculation with constant churn and no expansion. Observed renewal cohorts, actual founder time, channel costs and retention must replace these inputs before spending.

Run `python3 docs/growth/subscription-strategy-20261111/model.py` to reproduce the tables. Use `--report-cost 1.50` to change the base report cost. The model is an analysis artifact with no network or billing operations.
