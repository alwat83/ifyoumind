# Pricing experiment

**Proposed.** Use the founder-approved surface after its release decision and the [technical prerequisites](IMPLEMENTATION_BACKLOG.md).

## Audience and exposure

Recruit independent U.S. small-business location advisers and consultants with at least two city comparisons in a quarter and an active research decision. Require a supported city-level question. Exclude existing paying workspaces, duplicate accounts/organizations, staff, founder/QA/test, bots and unsupported use cases. Leave existing customers on their current terms. Qualify prospects before assignment and count every eligible exposed prospect, including those who leave before registration. Pending Founder Setup applies to invitations and other external distribution.

1. **Observe five relevant first-time visitors** using the [walkthrough](FUNNEL_RETENTION.md). Check that they can describe the product, inspect evidence, distinguish the personalized full report from the example, and explain renewal and credit expiry. If fewer than four can do those tasks unaided, fix the observed step before a paid comparison. Five sessions diagnose friction and provide qualitative evidence.
2. **Run a capped two-arm paid pilot** with separate founder approval after test verification. Arm A shows $4.99 Starter with one report. Arm B shows $3.99 Starter with one report. Both show $9.99 Plus with three reports. Keep free value, report quality, terms, channel and presentation identical, with the existing one-time path shown consistently. Allocate 1:1 using an approved, pseudonymous unique exposure key, then persist assignment to the eligible workspace at signup. Keep the originally agreed subscription price for enrolled accounts until they voluntarily change or separately approved transparent terms take effect.
3. **Enroll for 30 days**, extend once to a 60-day maximum if traffic is low, and close enrollment at 500 qualified exposed visitors or 60 settled subscriptions, whichever comes first. Follow each cohort for at least 35 days after enrollment closes, report 60/90-day retention as it matures, and keep access and billing support available. A pilot could use a manual, consented cohort register if anonymous assignment and denominators cannot be measured safely. Treat self-selected cohorts as descriptive evidence and avoid a causal price claim.

For a directional pricing decision require at least 100 unique qualified exposed visitors and 10 settled paid workspaces per arm with a first renewal opportunity. Small counts have wide uncertainty. For example, detecting a 10% to 15% conversion change at 80% power and a two-sided 5% test requires about 686 eligible prospects per arm under simple independent assignment. The proposed capped pilot is for directional evidence and customer learning. If the floor is unmet, publish counts and intervals where applicable, retain the control as the default proposal, and resolve the acquisition or comprehension gap before extending.

## Measurement

| Measure | Definition and source |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor → registration | Verified eligible workspaces started ÷ unique qualified visitors exposed, by assignment. Link via approved minimal assignment or manual cohort key. |
| Registration → paid | Workspaces with first settled subscription invoice ÷ verified eligible workspaces registered. Also report paid workspaces ÷ eligible visitors, by Starter and Plus. Exclude retries, QA and duplicate invoices. Track the one-time purchase path separately for substitution. |
| First full report | Workspaces with durable full-report unlock within seven days ÷ first paid workspaces observed for seven days. Deduplicate by project. Record time to usable report, errors and pending time. |
| Repeat | Paying workspaces starting a distinct supported decision on another day ÷ cohorts observed to day 60. Count reopens and prints separately. Report actual credits consumed per paid interval. |
| Retention | Settled second or third subscription invoices ÷ subscriptions with the corresponding renewal opportunity. Also show scheduled cancellation, voluntary and failed-payment churn separately. A saved report view is not a renewal. |
| Refund and dispute | Settled payments with full/partial refund or dispute ÷ settled payments in the observation window. Report counts, amount, unresolved timing and overlap without double-counting lost revenue. |
| Revenue per visitor | Settled subscription revenue net of refunds/dispute losses ÷ unique qualified exposed visitors for the fixed follow-up window. Show one-time revenue separately. |
| Contribution per visitor | Net settled pilot revenue less processing/Billing/dispute fees, report and support costs, and incremental pre-sale delivery costs for all exposed eligible traffic, divided by unique qualified exposed visitors. Use the same window for each cohort. Show subscription-only and combined results, subtract explicit acquisition spend separately and avoid double-counting in the report or free-use allocation. Also report contribution margin by plan against listed revenue for the modeled comparison. |

The current app captures authenticated workspace events for app open, decisions, report view, pricing, Checkout start/return and portal open. A Checkout return is a UX signal. Current admin metrics lack complete anonymous exposure, report activation, settled renewal, refund and usage attribution. Add only assignment/exposure, verified signup, first durable unlock and distinct use signals required to join payment and usage records. Reconcile fees, refunds and settled renewals against approved Stripe finance records. Use existing infrastructure. Use a short retention window for pseudonymous joins, restrict access, and delete experiment keys after reconciliation per approved privacy rules. Never send uploaded CSV values, project text, customer contact data or Stripe session URLs to analytics. [Measurement baseline](../MEASUREMENT_READINESS.md)

## Decision and stop rules

- **Continue $3.99** only with directionally higher contribution per eligible visitor, acceptable activation and repeat use, and no material decline in first renewal versus the control. At the current expected-use reserves, Starter-only contribution is about $2.62 at $3.99 versus $3.55 at $4.99. With the same mix, retention and costs, $3.99 needs about 36% higher Starter conversion to compensate. Use observed plan mix and uncertainty in the actual comparison. Require measured full-usage contribution of at least 60% of price for each price carried forward. [Model results](MODEL_OUTPUT.md)
- **Adjust** if the price is understood but first-report completion falls below the proposed 80% operational target, repeated use is rare, support is materially above the modeled reserve, or costs breach the guardrail. Observe the workflow and distinguish delivery, acquisition and repeat-demand problems before testing another offer. Review every early refund or dispute. Compare first-renewal, refund and support counts with exposure and intervals before inferring harm from small differences.
- **Hold or reject** the lower price if the observed contribution is lower, credible repeat demand is missing, or the minimum decision sample is unmet. Keep the public legacy path during evaluation. Immediately pause new enrollment for a cross-tenant grant, duplicate billing, unexpected renewal terms, missing purchased access, or inability to cancel. Preserve service and reconciliation for enrolled users.

Evidence for approval includes instrumented sandbox receipts, account portal cancellation, exact legacy access regressions, approved pilot terms and exposure handling, a verified cost worksheet with operator ownership, and a capped rollout and rollback checklist. Keep outcome labels “observed,” “modeled,” or “unknown.”
