# ifYouMind — Product experience blueprint

**Founder review proposal · November 14, 2026.** Preserve the existing product and release gates. The current mandate is to assess and plan; no product implementation or production change is initiated by this document.

**AI for everyone. AI made for you.** Turn real data into clear, personalized, evidence-backed conclusions that help people make better decisions. Personal means the question, inputs and limits belong to that decision. It does not require generating prose with a model.

## B. Unified experience blueprint

**Ask → Understand → Analyze → Explain → Decide.** Use a focused surface, one primary action, and the same evidence hierarchy across the site. Keep the original question visible where useful. Offer one clarification only when it changes the result; when the necessary evidence is unavailable, say what is missing and point to the next check. Keep examples, personal inputs and public estimates visually and semantically distinct.

| Visitor question | Smallest useful input | First useful answer, evidence and next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Why did my property tax increase?” | Production: two compatible redacted annual rows for the same property. Proposed #95 pilot: annual amount and year, with an optional earlier year; no address. | Show the observed change; show a line-item contribution only when the compatible breakdown exists. A single statement supports a budgeting fact, not a change. Disclose values and arithmetic, then suggest checking assessments, exemptions and rates through the relevant authority. |
| “How much of my electricity increase came from usage versus rates?” | Use billed kWh, current-period charges and comparable meter-read periods; known fixed/variable charges or official terms only when needed. Keep amounts and periods local. | Show change in billed usage and total charge; show an effective whole-bill cost per kWh only when meaningful. Do not call it a tariff. When rates, fees or compatible periods are missing, explicitly withhold a usage-versus-rate split. A future decomposition requires compatible line items or verified tariff evidence and documented math. |
| “What are the property taxes in Birmingham?” | Do not ask for a parcel or assume a state when the app has no applicable coverage. | #99 shows the evidence limit and directs the visitor to the jurisdiction/year and district source. No city Census proxy, parcel valuation or appeal promise. The made-up example is a separate choice. |
| “How do these cities compare for my decision?” | Ask for the city and state, a second city only for a comparison, and optional business context. Existing verified sign-in remains for a personalized market brief. | Present sourced Census findings, period and limits; keep the original question as context. Let visitors inspect the public sample before signup. After a useful brief, offer the existing report if its added evidence and analysis fit. |

**Across surfaces:** Homepage keeps one lead action and secondary routes; Discovery starts locally with the privacy notice before any bill input; decision creation keeps the question through correction and preserves only safe topic/offer intent through authentication; reports lead with a conclusion, the smallest set of supporting facts, and one next action. Pricing names precisely what the existing paid report adds; Account and saved decisions show actual access, source freshness where known, and recovery. Keep the existing tokens, typography and navigation conventions. Verify 320, 390, tablet and desktop, keyboard/touch, reduced motion and failure states; use reviewed snapshots and a physical-device spot check before release. Do not combine these surfaces into a new app shell or add a new dashboard.

**Trust and AI boundary:** Keep source, dates, units, comparison assumptions, deterministic calculations and uncertainty inspectable. AI may later explain verified facts or suggest a clarification, after quality and consent review. It must not change calculations or fill missing evidence. The first permitted real-data design, if approved, should use minimized report facts, validated structured output, an explicit data-handling review, bounded invocation and spending, and a deterministic fallback. Keep browser-local bill data out of provider requests. Never carry a question, bill identifiers or raw evidence into a URL or event payload.

## C. Prioritized roadmap

| Priority | Deliverable | Success signal and gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Immediate · relevance and first-use clarity** | Review #99 and observe the local-tax fallback, personal-bill route and public-to-market handoff. Prepare a lightweight walkthrough using synthetic data. Defer homepage wording changes until a repeated issue is observed. | Birmingham never shows a synthetic amount as its answer; user can name the missing evidence and choose a useful next step. Confirm before any exact-head release. |
| **Near term · easier personal value** | Reuse #95 for a separately reviewed typed-input pilot. Review how one statement gives a useful fact, a second strengthens it, and evidence and privacy remain visible. Prioritize a reliable first answer over new regions, PDFs or additional categories. | Observe a first meaningful finding and correct explanation of its limit on mobile. Verify no persistence or post-load requests, and run physical date-control and screen-reader checks. Rebase and review the existing draft only after scope approval. |
| **Growth · first independently verified purchase** | With permitted users who have a compatible market decision, use sample → useful free brief → relevant existing report offer → purchase → saved report. Record confusion and friction before changing anything. Review portal and enrollment separately through #84/#85. | Measure observed completion and reason to buy, then only verified independent payment plus report delivery and return. Track fees, refunds and attributable costs when available. No funnels, forecasts, or pricing changes from raw checkout events. |
| **Future · selective AI or new evidence** | Consider one fact-grounded market explanation through #96 after users demonstrate that reading evidence is the bottleneck. Consider one jurisdiction-specific tax source only if repeated real-user demand justifies its maintenance. | Compare correct understanding, evidence use, fallback rate, latency and cost against the deterministic experience; stop for unsupported claims, uncertain consent, cost drift or unmaintained sources. Provider use, data scope, source selection, and release each require their own approval. |

Ranking: #99 is a verified problem with low disruption. #95 offers more personal value but requires a deliberate review of a larger, older draft. A permitted first-use walkthrough has low engineering cost and may outrank copy changes. An AI or jurisdiction rollout has higher uncertainty and operational cost; defer until a measured need identifies the right constraint.

## D. Proposed draft PR sequence

1. **Relevance – keep #99.** Separate local tax, personal statement, parcel and tariff cases. Use its current scope and exact-head QA; smoke the incumbent examples, city flow and optional sample link with no request or private data transfer. Recheck/retain previous Hosting version before separate release approval. No duplicate relevance PR.
2. **First local result – keep #95 distinct.** After founder scope review, reconcile it with the current base without adding new intake. Verify exact calculations, mobile recovery, privacy text, zero network/storage, and unchanged examples and CSV. No dependency on #99 merging; coordinate entry copy after both drafts have known outcomes.
3. **Observed handoff friction – new PR only if warranted.** One repeated issue at a time, such as what an eligible report adds or where to find the private bill path. Use existing links and components, preserve auth/entitlements, and test sample → login/registration → brief → simulated checkout → entitlement → saved report. Use screenshots, reviewed baseline changes and exact-head CI. Any measured outcome is separate from QA.
4. **AI explanation – #96 remains its own gate.** Prepare only a separately reviewed synthetic evaluation or proposed report pilot if evidence warrants it. Preserve disabled staging, and keep deterministic report delivery independent. No AI work is started by this plan.

Initial walkthrough: with one consenting homeowner and one person with an active city decision when outreach is authorized, ask each to state the site's purpose without assistance, complete one useful task, explain one source or limitation, and identify their next step. Record anonymized counts, time to useful finding, first point of confusion, optional offer understanding, and reasons to return. This is qualitative, directional evidence. Avoid storing raw prompts/bills, adding events or treating test users as traffic or revenue. Outbound remains Pending Founder Setup.

If the correction is right, decide separately whether to authorize that exact release. Keep private bill evidence and the paid city/report experience clearly distinct.

Immediate recommendation: approve the product direction, review the existing #99 candidate, and observe first use before another homepage or architecture change.
