# Business usability study: founder approval package

**Status:** awaiting founder approval. Nobody has been contacted.

**Canonical copy:** Notion, "Business usability study: founder approval package" (child of the Command Center). Toolkit: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8faa5869-ecea-4e6a-a741-7af3cfdfec6a

**Approve:**
1. Channels.
2. The invitation wording as written.
3. The incentive.
4. The moderator.
5. The session window.

## Channels (no spend)
1. **Your own network:** people writing a plan now, or who write plans for others. This excludes friends, family and anyone close to ifYouMind or Dose Ninja, and never uses Dose Ninja contacts or mailboxes.
2. **Two adviser relationships** (SBDC, SCORE or independent plan writers): ask for a neutral forward only.
3. **One community post,** only if the quotas aren't met after a week and only where the rules allow research requests.

**Quotas:** 4 founders and 4 advisers; at least 3 on phones; at least 2 business types; a waitlist of 2.

## Invitation wording
Exact texts A (founder), B (adviser) and C (community post) are in the Notion page and the toolkit's "Outreach drafts" section.

**Rules:**
- One-to-one messages only.
- At most one follow-up, after 5 days.
- Stop at the first no.
- No claims about results, customers or AI.

## Moderator
- Reads the consent choice aloud (recorded or notes-only).
- Follows the script word for word and stays neutral.
- Uses the hint ladder only after 2 minutes stuck, and logs every hint.
- Never explains features, defends the price or offers discounts.
- Scores within 24 hours.

A second note-taker joins for notes-only sessions. Claude drafts the synthesis; the founder signs it off.

## Schedule
| Week | What |

| 1 | Dry run, then sessions 1–4 |
| 2 | Sessions 5–8 (at most 2 a day) |
| 3 | Synthesis within 3 days |

## Incentive (choose one)
- **None** ($0).
- **A free $79 report on their own cities after the session** ($0 cash; recommended; never counted as revenue).
- **A gift card:** not proposed, because spending isn't authorised.
