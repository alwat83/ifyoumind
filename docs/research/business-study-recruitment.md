## Final invitation drafts
**A. Founder (one-to-one)**
> **Subject:** 45 minutes of feedback on a business-plan research tool?
>
> Hi [name], I'm [your name] from ifYouMind, a small website that helps people write the market analysis section of a business plan using U.S. Census data. We're testing it with a handful of people who are writing a plan right now, and I'd value your honest reactions.
>
> It's a 45-minute video call. You'd try the site on your own business idea and cities while thinking aloud. There's nothing to buy, no sales pitch and no payment for taking part.
>
> If you're interested, a 2-minute form is here: [screener link]. If it isn't a fit, no reply is needed.
>
> Thanks, [your name] · ifYouMind · support@ifyoumind.com

**B. Adviser or plan writer (one-to-one)**
> **Subject:** Would you critique a market-analysis tool for 45 minutes?
>
> Hi [name], I saw [their public work]. I'm building ifYouMind, which compares U.S. cities on Census data and drafts a sourced market-analysis section. Before we go further, I'd like a professional's critique: is it accurate enough, and would it save you time?
>
> It's a 45-minute call. You'd try it on a real or anonymised client case. I'm not selling anything, and there's no payment for taking part.
>
> If you're open to it, here's a short form: [screener link]. If not, no reply is needed.
>
> [your name] · ifYouMind · support@ifyoumind.com

**B2. Adviser forward request (to an adviser you already know)**

If anyone comes to mind, would you be willing to forward the note below? There's no endorsement implied and no obligation either way.

> [Message A, with "Hi [name]" removed]

- One-to-one messages only, with at most one follow-up, and stop at the first no.
