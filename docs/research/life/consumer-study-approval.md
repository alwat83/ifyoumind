# ifYouMind Life: six-person validation study (founder approval package)

**Status:** awaiting founder approval. Nobody has been contacted, and nothing will be sent until you approve the channels, wording, sign-up form, moderator and launch.

**What participants use:** the private prototype (https://claude.ai/artifact/UPMV7T95KFu2zNEziiepXS), which uses illustrative figures. If the MVP route (PR L4) exists by then, the hidden build replaces it.

## Who (6 sessions plus a waitlist of 2)
- People who expect to compare a job offer, or a move between US cities, **within 6 months**.
- **At least 3 on phones**, and **at least 2 non-technical** participants.
- At least 2 who would decide **with a partner**, which tests the sharing hypothesis.
- **Excluded:** friends and family, anyone close to ifYouMind or Dose Ninja, and anyone recruited through Dose Ninja contacts, mailboxes or accounts.

## Channels (no spend)
1. **Your own network:** one-to-one messages.
2. **One community post** only if the quota isn't met after 7 days, and only where the rules allow research requests.

There is no paid advertising.

## Invitation (exact; no incentive line)
> **Subject:** 30 minutes to try a tool for comparing job offers?
>
> Hi [name], I'm [your name] from ifYouMind. We're testing a free tool that compares two job offers or cities using official data and your own numbers, such as salary, rent and commute. It shows what's an estimate and when there's no clear winner.
>
> It's a 30-minute video call where you try it, thinking aloud. You can use your own situation or a made-up one if you'd rather keep things private. There's nothing to buy and no payment for taking part.
>
> If you're interested, a 2-minute form is here: [screener link]. If it isn't a fit, no reply is needed.
>
> [your name] · ifYouMind · support@ifyoumind.com

**Follow-up:** one only, after 5 days: "Just checking this reached you. If now isn't a good time, no problem, and I won't follow up again."

## Screener (5 questions)
1. In the next 6 months, do you expect to compare a job offer or a move between US cities? (Yes / Maybe / No → No ends the form.)
2. Would you decide alone or with someone else?
3. Which device would you use for the call?
4. How comfortable are you with spreadsheets or online calculators? (1–5)
5. Do you work for, or know anyone at, ifYouMind or Dose Ninja? (Yes ends the form.)

## Session (30 minutes, moderated)
- **Consent:** read aloud. Recorded, or notes only, as the participant chooses. Participant ids only.
- **Data:** participants may use a made-up scenario. The moderator never asks for real salary figures to be shared aloud.

| Task | Prompt | Pass (per participant) | Study pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 · 10-second test | Show `/life` for 10 s: "What does this do? Who is it for?" | Names comparing jobs or cities with their own numbers | ≥ 5 of 6 |
| C2 · Complete unaided | "Compare your two options." | Reaches the verdict in under 8 minutes with at most 1 hint | ≥ 4 of 6 |
| C3 · Interpret | "Which numbers are facts, which are estimates, which are yours? What did it conclude?" | All three labelled correctly, and the verdict (including "no clear winner") stated | ≥ 4 of 6 |
| C4 · Useful and trusted | Ratings from 1 to 5, and why | 4 or more on both | ≥ 4 of 6 |
| C5 · Share | "Would you send this to anyone? Show me what you'd send." | Uses the copy summary, and names a person | ≥ 3 of 6 |
| C6 · Return and next | "When would you come back? What other decision would you use this for? Would you pay for anything here?" | Recorded verbatim | Inputs only; never counted as demand |

## Moderator
- **Sessions 1 and 2:** you. Sessions 3–6: you, or someone you name.
- The moderator follows the script, stays neutral, uses the hint ladder only after 2 minutes stuck, and scores within 24 hours.
- Claude drafts the synthesis; you sign it off.
