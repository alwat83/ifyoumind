What’s worth checking?

Check the assessment notice and exemption entries for both tax years. The billed amounts alone cannot tell us whether either changed. This preview does not analyze those documents.

Look for official rate notices and the same taxing authorities in both years. A matching percentage change is not proof of a rate change. No cause has been verified here.

Match the tax year, property class, assessment basis, exemptions and taxing jurisdictions before comparing. Nearby bills or city averages may not be comparable. This preview does not fetch other properties or recommend a cheaper place.
