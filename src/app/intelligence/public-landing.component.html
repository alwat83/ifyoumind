How ifYouMind Works

A simple process from question to conclusion.

  1. 1. Ask a Question

    Choose a business location or compare the U.S. cities you’re considering.

  2. 2. Analyze the Data

    See how your options compare using U.S. Census estimates, with sources attached.

  3. 3. Get a Clear Report

    Read the trade-offs, see the limits, and get a practical list of what to check next.
