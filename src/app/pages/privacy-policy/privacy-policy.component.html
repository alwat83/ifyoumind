What ifYouMind is

ifYouMind is a data and decision-intelligence product operated by Dose Ninja Inc. It helps users create workspaces, import or connect data, analyze relationships, compare markets, ask questions about available data, and generate decision-oriented analysis.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name and email address, workspace and project content you provide, datasets or files you upload, connection metadata for services you choose to connect, product usage information, diagnostic logs, and billing metadata when paid services are enabled.

Your datasets and analysis

Data you provide may be processed to deliver the analyses, comparisons, reports, and AI-assisted explanations you request. We do not treat uploaded customer datasets as public content. Access is limited according to workspace authorization and service requirements.

AI processing

Some features may send a limited evidence packet or other necessary context to an AI provider to generate an explanation. The product is designed to separate underlying statistical evidence from generative explanation. Provider usage may change as the product evolves.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, authenticate users, operate workspaces, run requested analysis, support users, improve reliability and product experience, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers

We may use infrastructure, authentication, analytics, AI, payment, email, and data-provider services to operate ifYouMind. Those providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for the services they perform, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

We use essential browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your cookie choice. Only if you choose "Allow analytics", we also use Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC through Firebase, to understand how visitors use ifYouMind: for example which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, and basic device, browser and approximate location information that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (such as _ga) for this. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not send your name, email address, uploaded data or report contents to it.

If you have not made a choice, or chose "Reject", Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time; withdrawing stops collection straight away. Google describes how it uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Payments

When billing is enabled, payment card details will be handled by the payment processor rather than stored directly by ifYouMind. We may retain limited billing information such as plan, payment status, and transaction identifiers.

Retention and security
